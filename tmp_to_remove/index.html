About Me

I am an Associate Professor at UiT The Arctic University of Norway.

I hold a PhD in Information Engineering & Science from the University of Siena. My research focuses on Graph Neural Networks (GNNs), specifically investigating their expressivity, temporal graph processing, and link prediction capabilities.
